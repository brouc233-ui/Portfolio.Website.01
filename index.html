About Me

I’m Conrad—a designer, illustrator, and creative coder based in New York. I spend my time making logos, brand systems, album covers, detailed architectural pen-and-ink drawings, portraits, and interactive websites.

Most of my work starts on paper or in a sketchbook before moving into digital tools or code. Whether I’m spending months drawing stone cathedrals and world monuments in ink, building a visual identity from scratch, or coding a browser instrument that turns typing into music, I care about patience, clear structure, and making things that feel personal.
